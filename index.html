Where it stands

What it does

  • It reads what is on your screen, not a picture of it
  • It does one thing at a time
  • It checks the screen again before moving on

What it cannot do yet

  • It cannot use websites yet. It opens your browser, it does not drive the page.
  • It will never type a password. It stops and hands the keyboard back to you.
  • Windows only for now.
  • It is not finished. You are joining a waitlist, not buying a product.
