Hue 207 sits clear of Microsoft blue (205 at low value), Alexa cyan (190), and the blue-violet Siri and Gemini both use. High in value so it still holds at 16px, where a deep blue turns to mud.

How it is built

The body is solidAn opaque dark disc. It is the object, not a window.
The ring and dot are glassTinted transparent, set into the body with a shadow just outside and just inside them.
The blades run underneathThey pass below the glass ring and stay visible through it. That is what proves the ring is transparent rather than painted on, and they end on the inner circle.
Depth is at the edgeA rim hairline catching light top-left and again weaker bottom-right, a dark line inside it for glass thickness, and a contact shadow. No bevel in the field.

The small mark is not the master shrunk. Its ring and dot are enlarged and its blades, glow and sheen are removed, because at 32px the real proportions turn to a smudge. Same silhouette, different optics. This is deliberate.

Rules

  • Clear space on every side is one dot diameter. Nothing enters it.
  • Do not use the master below 96px. Switch to the small mark.
  • Do not make the ring opaque. The glass reading depends on the blades showing through it.
  • Do not recolour the ring. The state colours below belong to the live indicator, never to the logo.
  • Do not add a drop shadow. There is already a contact shadow in the artwork.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or crop the disc.
